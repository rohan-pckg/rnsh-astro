I use AI a lot when I talk to people. Not in the middle of the conversation, but around it: before I reply to something hard, or when I can't find the words for what I mean.

The test I keep coming back to is simple. Could I say this out loud, to their face, and sound like myself?

If the answer is no, the message isn't ready, however polished it looks.

What helps me is to write the messy version first, in my own words, with the small mistakes in. Then I ask the tool where I'm unclear, or what I forgot to say. It's good at that. It spots the gap. It is much worse at sounding like me, so I fill the gap myself.

People can tell. Not always how, but they feel it when a message arrives smoothed over and slightly far away. The rough edges are part of how we know someone is actually there.

So I let AI help me think, and I keep the talking for myself. If something matters, I show up as me, a little unfinished, and send it anyway.
